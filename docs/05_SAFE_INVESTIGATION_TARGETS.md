# 🎯 Section 05: 25 Safe & Legal Investigation Scenarios

**Standard Reference:** `OSINT-LAB-SEC-05`  
**Classification:** `PUBLIC / TLP:CLEAR`  

---

## 🛡️ Target Selection Methodology

All exercises in this curriculum use **100% legal, non-intrusive target categories**. At no point will a student investigate a living individual without explicit consent, probe unauthorized networks, or bypass technical controls.

---

## 📋 The 25 Vetted Target Categories

### Category A: Public Regulatory & Corporate Filings (SEC EDGAR)
1. **Target 01:** Fortune 500 Infrastructure Footprint (SEC Form 10-K Exhibit 21 - List of Subsidiaries).
2. **Target 02:** Executive Board Disclosures & Cross-Corporate Directorship Mapping.
3. **Target 03:** Mergers & Acquisitions Asset Identification (Form 8-K Disclosures).
4. **Target 04:** Cloud Provider Dependencies in Risk Disclosures (Item 1A Cybersecurity Disclosures).
5. **Target 05:** Foreign Entity Registration & Trademark Tracking (USPTO / WIPO Databases).

### Category B: Open-Source Software Repositories & Foundations
6. **Target 06:** The Apache Software Foundation (Subdomain & mirror network mapping).
7. **Target 07:** Linux Foundation Project Ecosystem (Git author metadata & commit timeline analysis).
8. **Target 08:** Wikimedia Foundation (Certificate transparency & DNS infrastructure analysis).
9. **Target 09:** Mozilla Public Suffix List & Root Store Metadata.
10. **Target 10:** Open-Source Dependency Security Advisories (PyPI / npm package provenance).

### Category C: Public Certificate Transparency & DNS Infrastructure
11. **Target 11:** Passive TLS/SSL Certificate Logs via `crt.sh` and Censys Search.
12. **Target 12:** Cloud Provider ASN & IP Range Mapping (AWS IP Ranges JSON / Cloudflare ASNs).
13. **Target 13:** DNS Mail Hardening Verification (SPF, DKIM, and DMARC enforcement records).
14. **Target 14:** Expired Domain & Abandoned DNS Record Identification (Dangling CNAMEs).
15. **Target 15:** Historical DNS Drift Analysis via SecurityTrails / ViewDNS.info.

### Category D: Web Archives & Historical Analysis
16. **Target 16:** Wayback Machine (Archive.org) Corporate Restructuring Analysis.
17. **Target 17:** Historical Job Posting Footprints (Identifying legacy tech stack requirements via archived hiring portals).
18. **Target 18:** Privacy Policy & Terms of Service Evolution Tracking.
19. **Target 19:** Robots.txt & Sitemap Historical Exposure Mapping.
20. **Target 20:** Public Media & Press Release Disinformation Verification.

### Category E: Synthetic CTF & Defensible Threat Intelligence Datasets
21. **Target 21:** CyberDefenders Open-Source Intelligence CTF Challenges.
22. **Target 22:** SANS Holiday Hack Challenge Historical OSINT Archives.
